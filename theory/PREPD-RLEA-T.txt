       because they review original studies

     Literature Reviews
     Goals of a Literature Review
     1. Provide a solid theoretical foundation for the proposed
        study in the dissertation course (related to “what is
        already known?”)
     2. To help the student understand the existing body of
        knowledge including where excess research exists (i.e.
        what is already know?) and where new research is needed
        (i.e. what is needed to be known?)
     3. Substantiate the presence of the research problem
        (related to “what is needed to be known?”)
     4. Frame the valid research methodologies, approach, goals,
        and research questions for the proposed study
        (dissertation)
     5. In certain cases, justify the proposed study as one that
        contributes something new to the body of knowledge
                                                                      Adapted from
                                                                      [Levy&Ellis, 2006]

     Literature Reviews
     Systematic Literature Review (SLR)
     SLR
     Definition: A systematic literature review is a formalized
     method to address a specific question by analyzing
     the breadth of published literature while minimizing bias
     (adapted from [Smith et al., 2022] )




     • “address a specific question” is a concrete test
                                                          Example: does drug A cures disease X
     • However, this is not always needed
     • MEI/PREPD is such a case, where the goal of the
       review is not to answer a specific question

     Literature Reviews
     Mapping study (MS)                                                                   The method we
                                                                                          are going to use
     Mapping Study (MS)
     Definition: A method to provide a wide overview of a
     research area, to establish if exists research evidence
     on a topic and provide an indication of the quantity of
     the evidence

     • Also know as systematic mapping study, systematic
       scoping review, or simply a mapping study (MS)

     • Can be more or less “rapid” depending on the
       constraints of time and resources. More rapid when
       the area, or the situation, changes fast.
